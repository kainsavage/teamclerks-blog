I have written a bit about my love of FromSoftware games and how I got into them after their peak.
I played Sekiro in 2022 after beating Elden Ring and finding the gameplay, story, music... basically
everything amazing. I have not written about my love of Elden Ring, but I have 1,300+ hours in the
game between regular runs, themed runs, challenge runs, and randomizer runs. Suffice it to say that
the game grabbed me and held on tightly.

I have 600+ hours in Nightreign and I don't see an end in sight. It pulls me back like Elden Ring on
steroids. Everything I loved about randomizers is what makes the game enjoyable: random loot, random
teammates, random perks, random relics (more on this later), random bosses... and a goal. See, you
can't "win" Nightreign anymore than you can win any 
[roguelite](https://plarium.com/en/glossary/roguelite/); rather, FromSoft added a mode called "Deep
of Night" in which you gain and lose points for wins and losses. There are five depths, and each one
gets subsequently more difficult starting with D1 which is pretty much normal gameplay but unlocks
the use of 3 additional DoN-only relics. At 200 points per win, most players reach depth 2 (1,000 
points) pretty quickly. Depth 2 introduces red enemies and items which are stronger than their 
normal counterparts, but will have curses attached to their buffs. For instance, you might pick up
a red item with +10% Max HP, but it might also have Poison Buildup When Below Max HP. Once you get
to 2,000 rating you enter Depth 3 and the incoming damage and enemy health pools explode. Also, the
grind gets longer: in order to get to Depth 4, you must achieve 4,000 rating. And on and on it 
goes... _except_ Depth 5 starts at 6,000 and goes to 9,999.

Four nines is the goal. First off, fewer than 3% of the playerbase has made it to depth 5 (at least
that is what Bandai Namco reported in January 2025 in their 
[Stats From the Night](https://en.bandainamcoent.eu/elden-ring/news/more-numbers-the-night-the-latest-stats-elden-ring-nightreign))
blog post. We can extrapolate that even fewer make it to 9,999.
